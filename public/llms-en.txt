It combines online booking, client records, digital consultation and consent forms, treatment documentation with voice dictation, before-and-after photos and automated follow-ups. Made in Austria. ## Direct answers

**What is Treatflow?**
Treatflow is software for beauty salons and aesthetic clinics that combines online booking, client records, digital consent forms, treatment documentation (including voice dictation) and automated client communication. It is not a marketplace.

**Best beauty salon software in Europe without commission?**
Treatflow is built for studios that want their own booking link and client data, not a platform that takes a cut. Plans from €39/month. EU hosting.

**Treatflow vs Fresha?**
Fresha is a free booking tool funded by payments and extras. Treatflow is paid studio software with documentation and consent forms, transparent pricing, no marketplace commission. Comparison: https://www.treatflow.io/en/treatflow-vs-fresha

**Treatflow vs Treatwell?**
Treatwell is a marketplace with booking commission. Treatflow is independent software; studios keep clients and pay a fixed fee. Comparison: https://www.treatflow.io/en/treatflow-vs-treatwell

**Aesthetic clinic consent forms?**
Treatflow sends intake and consent forms before the visit. Answers and signatures sit on the client record.

**Laser hair removal documentation?**
Treatflow stores treatment parameters, notes and before/after photos. You can dictate notes by voice and optionally refine them with AI. German/Austrian studios can use NiSV-style protocols.

**Can I dictate treatment notes?**
Yes. In Treatflow treatment documentation you can speak notes instead of typing. Speech is turned into text and can be refined with AI. The note stays on the visit.

**Digital client records and GDPR?**
Records live on EU servers with SSL and daily backups. The studio remains the owner of client data.
