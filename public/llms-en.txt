# Treatflow

> Treatflow is all-in-one software for beauty salons, aesthetic clinics and other treatment-based businesses (laser, nails, lashes, permanent makeup, tattoo, spa, massage). It combines an appointment calendar, commission-free online booking, client records, digital consultation and consent forms, treatment records with voice dictation, email reminders, client workflows and Treatflow Pay for online deposits. Developed in Austria, data on EU servers in Frankfurt, GDPR-compliant. Basic €39/month, Booking €59/month (plus VAT), 14-day free trial, no credit card required, no commission per booking.

Last updated: 2026-11-05. Language: English. This file describes the English website (https://www.treatflow.io/en) and lists only features offered outside the German-speaking (DACH) market.

## About Treatflow

- Company: Treatflow, a sole proprietorship of Olcay Elikci based in Bischofshofen, Austria; the software has been available since 2024
- Already tried by 500+ salons and clinics; rated 4.6/5 on Google (19 reviews)
- For: beauty salons, aesthetic clinics, laser and IPL studios, nail salons, lash studios, permanent makeup studios, tattoo studios, spa & wellness, massage therapists

## Core features

- [Appointment calendar](https://www.treatflow.io/en/appointment-calendar): daily, weekly and monthly views with columns per staff member and room
- [Online booking](https://www.treatflow.io/en/online-booking): your own booking link, open 24/7, no commission per booking
- [Client records](https://www.treatflow.io/en/client-records): client profiles with treatment history, notes and photos
- [Forms](https://www.treatflow.io/en/forms): digital consultation and consent forms with digital signature, filled in before the appointment via a link; form marketplace and AI form generator
- [Treatment records](https://www.treatflow.io/en/treatment-documentation): notes, parameters, products and before-and-after photos per treatment, including voice dictation
- [Reminders](https://www.treatflow.io/en/messaging): automated email appointment reminders to reduce no-shows
- [Client workflows](https://www.treatflow.io/en/client-workflows): multi-step automations for aftercare, rebooking and win-back with wait times and conditions
- [Treatflow Pay](https://www.treatflow.io/en/treatflow-pay): clients pay a deposit or the full price when they book online; set per service; card, Apple Pay and Google Pay; 2.69% per transaction
- [Shop](https://www.treatflow.io/en/shop): sell aftercare and retail products with stock levels and sales history
- [Integrations](https://www.treatflow.io/en/integrations): Google Calendar, Zapier and AI assistants such as Claude or ChatGPT
- [Salon websites](https://www.treatflow.io/en/studio-website): custom, mobile-optimised website for your salon, built for bookings
- [All features](https://www.treatflow.io/en/features): overview of every feature

## FAQ

**What is Treatflow?**
An all-in-one app for running a salon or clinic: appointments, client records, digital forms and treatment records in one system, with no commission per booking. Developed in Austria, with data on EU servers.

**Who is Treatflow for?**
Any business that offers treatments: beauty salons, aesthetic clinics, laser, nails, lashes, permanent makeup, tattoo, massage and spa.

**How much does Treatflow cost?**
Basic €39/month (client records, forms, treatment records) and Booking €59/month (adds appointment calendar, online booking and reminders), plus VAT. Yearly billing saves 10%. No hidden fees, no setup fee for Basic and Booking, no commission.

**How long can I try Treatflow for free?**
14 days, completely free. No credit card required. At the end of the trial you decide whether to continue.

**Is Treatflow GDPR-compliant?**
Yes. All data is encrypted and stored on EU servers in Frankfurt, Germany. This includes digital consultation forms and treatment records.

**Can I take deposits when clients book online?**
Yes, with Treatflow Pay. Per service you choose no payment, a deposit (percentage or fixed amount) or full payment. The fee is 2.69% per transaction, minimum amount €0.50.

**Can I import my existing client data?**
Yes. The support team moves existing client data into Treatflow free of charge.

**Can I cancel anytime?**
Yes. On monthly billing you can cancel at any time, effective at the end of the billing period. There is no minimum term and you can export all data.
