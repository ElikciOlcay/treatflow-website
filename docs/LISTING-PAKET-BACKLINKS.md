## Lange Beschreibung (DE)

Treatflow ist die All-in-One Studio-Software für Kosmetikstudios, Beauty-Salons, Nagel-, Lash- und Laserstudios. Mit Treatflow planst du Termine, nimmst Online-Buchungen entgegen, führst eine digitale Kundenkartei, versendest Anamnese- und Einwilligungsformulare und dokumentierst Behandlungen – inklusive NiSV-konformer Protokolle. Treatflow is all-in-one salon software for beauty and cosmetics studios. Schedule appointments, accept online bookings, manage digital client records, send intake and consent forms, and document treatments – including compliance-ready protocols. An optional TSE/RKSV-ready POS module is available without transaction fees.

Built in Austria, GDPR-compliant EU hosting, focused on studios in Germany, Austria and Switzerland. 14-day free trial, no credit card required.
